# Beanie

Async MongoDB ODM (Object-Document Mapper) for Python, built on top of [[Motor]] and Pydantic. Models subclass `Document` and declare collections via `class Settings`. All models must be registered in `backend/app/db/session.py`. See [[Beanie ODM]] and [[Beanie Document Pattern]] for usage.
